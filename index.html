About the Club

Not a Lecture.
A Conversation.

What

The club helps students develop a further understanding of artificial intelligence: the tools available, and how we can use them responsibly and effectively in the architecture profession.

Why

AI is now everywhere and affects all of us. We want a place where we can explore and share workflows, tools and the best methods of using AI in an ethical way.

Who

Open to anyone in the WLVArchitecture community, at any level of experience.

How Sessions Work

Discuss — bring a question, a view or something you have read

Test — try tools and workflows on real project work, together

Share — what worked and what didn't, then write it up on this site

This is not a lecture, so come prepared to discuss, test and share.
