## Reports and professional bodies

- [**RIBA Artificial Intelligence Report 2026**](https://www.riba.org/work/insights-and-resources/ai-report/) — Royal Institute of British Architects
  RIBA's third annual AI report draws on its largest member survey to date, showing that 74% of practices now use AI on at least some projects, from early-stage visualisation through to project and practice management. It also sets out the profession's concerns, including environmental impact, intellectual property and the effect on the career path of those starting out, which makes it essential reading for anyone entering practice.
