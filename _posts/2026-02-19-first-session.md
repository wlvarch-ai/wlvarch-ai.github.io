## Discussed

The first WLVArch.AI session asked a simple question: what would help students use AI well in their architecture studies? Members raised a range of issues they thought deserved more time in the club, and we captured them on the whiteboard.

The list, as written on the whiteboard:

- Identify the risks of using AI
- Assist with essays - How can it help but not be used as cheating
- Give feedback on my own work
- Best way to summarise documents or pieces of work to make them easy to digest
- Different tools available and how we know which are best to use
- Understanding how they work - Not sure how various AI tools work or what the different terms mean
- Environmental impact - what is it and how might anything I do impact the environment


## Next time

These topics give the club its starting agenda. If you want to add to the list, or to lead a session on one of these topics, bring it to the next meeting.
